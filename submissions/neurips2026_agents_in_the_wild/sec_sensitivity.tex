\paragraph{Sensitivity and ablations.}
Holding the fixture suite fixed, we vary one Risk Engine parameter at a time around
\texttt{balanced} $(\kappa,\gamma,\tau,R_{\mathrm{MAX}})=(2.0,0.8,1.5,1.5)$ and report
stateful attack catch / FN, benign FP, and veto step on \texttt{salami\_original} /
\texttt{salami\_compositional}.
Reproduce with \texttt{python3 auto\_test/test\_risk\_sensitivity.py}.

\noindent\textit{Takeaway.}
On these fixtures, compositional catch is most sensitive to $\tau$ and $R_{\mathrm{MAX}}$
(and to lowering $\kappa$); $\gamma{=}0$ alone still vetoes because $\tau\cdot\Delta R$
on the final spike remains large.
Benign FP stays $0$ near \texttt{balanced}.
Strict-like thresholds veto earlier (step~2) while still counting as attack prevention.
Profiles remain hand-chosen heuristics, not fitted estimators.
